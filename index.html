SchemeSathi Solution Architecture & Impact Matrix

Domain: Civic Tech / AI for Bharat / DPI

Problem Solved

India operates over 1,000 welfare schemes with massive budgets, but over 40% of eligible beneficiaries fail to access them due to technical jargon, lack of centralized eligibility logic, and last-mile document bottlenecks.

AI & Tech Innovation

• RAG + LLM Translation: Bureaucratic gazette notifications converted into 5th-grade reading level.
• Multi-criteria Rule Engine: Instant matching across income, demographic, and profession parameters.
• Interactive Document Readiness: Step-by-step CSC/MeeSeva actionable slips.

Scalability & Future Scope

• Bhashini API Voice Integration: Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi dialect voice bot.
• WhatsApp & SMS Gateway: Zero-smartphone accessibility via simple WhatsApp messaging.
• DigiLocker Integration: 1-click automatic document verification without physical visits.
